# Part 1 — Creative direction

## 1.1 The campaign idea: "The Quiet Interior"

HUMAN is about what is inside a person and not yet visible. The campaign never illustrates that with anatomy. It uses **interiors**: thresholds, courts, stairs, verandahs, rooms where light arrives through something (a silk sheer, a carved screen, ribbed glass, a skylight, a glass of water, the surface of a pool). In every picture, light has passed through or bounced off a material before it reaches the subject. That is the single recurring motif, and it is the photographic twin of the Reveal Lens on the site.

The setting language is **Indian modernism and its restored predecessors**: board-formed concrete, exposed brick, lime plaster, Kota and Kadappa stone, red oxide floors, terrazzo, teak, ash, cane, bell metal, bronze. These are real materials with visible joints, wear and repair. Nothing is a showroom.

## 1.2 Colour system

| Role | Colour | Where it is allowed to live |
|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | Rich charcoal #0B0D0F to basalt | Concrete in shadow, stone, deep shade |
| Ground (light) | Natural ivory #F6F3EC | Lime plaster, pale concrete, cotton, terrazzo |
| Accent | Champagne amber, restrained | One element per image at most: a sun blade, bell metal, a lamp |
| Her world | Garnet #54263B, mulberry, oxblood | Pigmented plaster, dyed silk, red oxide floor |
| His world | Deep ocean teal #164846, slate | Dyed linen, tinted plaster, stone, water-light |
| Skin | Neutral-warm, true to the individual | Never tinted by the grade |

Rules: colour is carried by materials, never by a wash or a gel. Amber appears as one bounded element and is absent from four of the ten images. Garnet and teal appear only in the two portals; the story images stay in charcoal, ivory and natural material colour so the portals arrive as an event.

## 1.3 Light

One motivated source per picture, named in the brief, with its direction and what it has passed through. Fill comes only from surfaces in the room. No rim light without a physical cause. No haze, no visible rays, no glow behind a head. Shadows keep detail and take their colour from the nearest surface.

## 1.5 Casting

**Direction.** Every adult is a fictional contemporary urban Indian, 24 to 35, naturally attractive with distinctive and harmonious features, athletic and visibly healthy, well-groomed and self-assured. Fitness shows in posture, shoulders, forearms and how clothes sit, never in flexing or exposed muscle. Nobody looks at the camera. Nobody performs health, wealth or beauty.

**Men:** lean and muscular with naturally developed shoulders and arms; clean modern grooming; calm, not aggressive. **Women:** fit and athletic with elegant natural proportions; sophisticated grooming; relaxed, not posed.

**Ten different people.** No two share a hair shape, a skin tone and a build. Each has two or three named features.

## 1.6 Wardrobe

Premium and understated, with excellent fit. Tailoring in silk crêpe, linen-silk twill, khadi-silk, wool crêpe and tropical wool; knitwear in merino, cotton-silk and cotton-cashmere; luxury activewear in heavyweight cotton-modal, fine merino jersey and matte technical twill. Each garment is described by cut, cloth and how it creases. Clothes follow the body where a real tailor would let them and stay modest everywhere. Colour sits inside the palette. No logos, no trend pieces, no festive or bridal styling, no watches or jewellery used as status, no bare chests, no compression wear.

## 1.7 Photography

Medium-format rendering throughout, with the lens and camera height chosen per picture: 35–45 mm with shift for architecture, 55–80 mm for figures in rooms, 90–110 mm for the still life and the portrait. Verticals are straight in every architectural frame. Grain is fine and organic. Retouching restraint is part of the look: pores, fine skin texture, flyaway hairs and creased cloth stay.

## 1.8 Lifestyle

Affluence is carried by the places and the making, never by objects that announce money. The settings are architect-designed private residences (the doorway, the water court, the courtyard dining room, the daybed room, the screened gallery, the kitchen counter), a private wellness building (the track, the stair, the pool loggia) and a design studio. What reads as expensive is board-marked concrete poured well, a teak screen with even fins, hand-beaten bell metal, a stone counter, tailored cloth. Excluded everywhere: cars, logos, watches as status, mansions, chandeliers, marble lobbies, displayed jewellery.

## 1.9 Sequence on the homepage

Hero (dark) → **care-walk** (bright, wide) with **care-meal** (dark, graphic) and **care-rest** (dusk) → **progress-stride** (darkest, vertical) → Reveal Lens (coded, optional macro) → **feeling-fine** (brightest) → Scene 05 and 06 (coded) → **journey** trio (cool studio, warm gallery, fresh portrait) → Scene 08 (coded) → **portal-her** and **portal-him** (first appearance of garnet and teal).

## PORTAL_HER — “Threshold” · `portal-her.png`

**Canvas:** 3:4 portrait, 1800×2400 px minimum; ask for the largest 3:4 offered.  
**Website placement:** Homepage Her/Him portals, left panel (636×738 px at 1440; 350×560 px at 390). Also the right 44% of the /women hero (about 634×830 px).

**01 — Creative concept.** A woman paused in a deep doorway between a dark room and a lit one. The garnet is physical: pigment in lime plaster and dye in silk, never a colour wash over the picture. This is the key visual of the campaign; every later image is judged against it.

**02 — Visual storytelling.** She was on her way through and has stopped to think. The viewer should read self-possession, health and an interior life, and should feel invited across the same threshold. She is not posing and nothing suggests a patient, a bride or a model at work.

**03 — Subject casting.** Fictional Indian woman, 31–34. Tall, with an athletic, slim build: a swimmer’s shoulders, toned forearms, long neck, upright and easy posture. Deep warm-brown skin with a healthy, matte finish. High cheekbones, a strong straight brow, a slightly aquiline nose. Dark hair, centre-parted, drawn into a sleek low knot. Attention directed out of frame to camera-right, mouth closed, the start of amusement at one corner.

**04 — Wardrobe and styling.** An impeccably cut long shirt-dress in heavy washed silk crêpe, deep garnet (#54263B), matte with a dry sheen only along folds. Precise shoulder seams, sharp collar open one button, sleeves rolled twice to show the forearms, self-fabric belt tied at the natural waist so the cloth falls in a clean column with real creases at elbow and waist. One slim hammered-gold cuff on the right wrist, small gold studs, a plain tortoiseshell pin in the hair. Grooming: brows brushed, skin bare-looking, lips natural. Excluded: necklace, scarf, bridal or festive styling, visible make-up, nail colour.

**05 — Environment and set design.** A restored early-modern Indian house. A deep doorway, wall about 45 cm thick, in hand-trowelled lime plaster pigmented oxblood-mulberry, with visible trowel movement and a slight burnish. Door frame in old oiled teak. A brass lever handle with worn lacquer. Floor in polished dark red oxide with hairline craquelure. Through the doorway to the right, a brighter room with a tall narrow window behind an undyed raw-silk sheer. No furniture, no ornaments.

**06 — Composition and framing.** Vertical 3:4. She stands in the doorway in the right 55% of the frame, three-quarter length (head to mid-thigh), left shoulder resting on the teak frame, right hand on the frame edge at chest height. Top of head 12% from the top; eyes 26–30% down and 60–64% across. The left 34% is uninterrupted dark plaster. The bottom 36% falls to deep shadow: dress and floor in low values, nothing bright. The site crops about 4% off the top and 9% off the bottom on desktop, and about 10% off the left and 7% off the right on mobile, so head and both hands stay inside the central zone. Live text sits bottom-left.

**07 — Camera direction.** Medium format, 80 mm lens (about 63 mm full-frame). Camera level at her sternum height, 2.6 m away, verticals straight. f/4. Focus on the near eye; plaster texture beside her stays legible, the far room goes soft.

**08 — Lighting design.** One source: daylight from the tall window in the far room, diffused by the silk sheer, arriving from camera-right and slightly behind her (about 110° off the lens axis). It draws the edge of her profile, the collarbone and the sleeve folds, and lays one soft-edged vertical band of light on the plaster behind her shoulder. Fill is only bounce from the red floor and wall, about two stops under. 5200 K. No lamp, no second rim.

**09 — Colour grading.** Low-key with open shadows. Blacks sit at deep aubergine, not zero. The dress is slightly more saturated than the wall so the cloth leads. Skin neutral-warm with true brown undertones and matte highlights. Medium contrast, no added vignette.

**10 — Microdetails.** Trowel marks catching the light band; one hairline crack in the plaster; dry sheen along a single silk fold; a few fine flyaway hairs at the temple; the tendon line of her forearm where the sleeve is rolled; a scuff on the brass handle; the window as a faint soft bar reflected in the polished floor at far right.
