# Part 1 — Creative direction

## 1.1 The campaign idea: "The Quiet Interior"

HUMAN is about what is inside a person and not yet visible. The campaign never illustrates that with anatomy. It uses **interiors**: thresholds, courts, stairs, verandahs, rooms where light arrives through something (a silk sheer, a carved screen, ribbed glass, a skylight, a glass of water, the surface of a pool). In every picture, light has passed through or bounced off a material before it reaches the subject. That is the single recurring motif, and it is the photographic twin of the Reveal Lens on the site.

The setting language is **Indian modernism and its restored predecessors**: board-formed concrete, exposed brick, lime plaster, Kota and Kadappa stone, red oxide floors, terrazzo, teak, ash, cane, bell metal, bronze. These are real materials with visible joints, wear and repair. Nothing is a showroom.

## 1.2 Colour system

| Role | Colour | Where it is allowed to live |
|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | Rich charcoal #0B0D0F to basalt | Concrete in shadow, stone, deep shade |
| Ground (light) | Natural ivory #F6F3EC | Lime plaster, pale concrete, cotton, terrazzo |
| Accent | Champagne amber, restrained | One element per image at most: a sun blade, bell metal, a lamp |
| Her world | Garnet #54263B, mulberry, oxblood | Pigmented plaster, dyed silk, red oxide floor |
| His world | Deep ocean teal #164846, slate | Dyed linen, tinted plaster, stone, water-light |
| Skin | Neutral-warm, true to the individual | Never tinted by the grade |

Rules: colour is carried by materials, never by a wash or a gel. Amber appears as one bounded element and is absent from four of the ten images. Garnet and teal appear only in the two portals; the story images stay in charcoal, ivory and natural material colour so the portals arrive as an event.

## 1.3 Light

One motivated source per picture, named in the brief, with its direction and what it has passed through. Fill comes only from surfaces in the room. No rim light without a physical cause. No haze, no visible rays, no glow behind a head. Shadows keep detail and take their colour from the nearest surface.

## 1.6 Wardrobe

Natural fibres with visible weight: silk crêpe, linen-silk twill, khadi-silk, handloom cotton, loopback cotton, merino, tropical wool. Each garment is described by cut, cloth and how it creases. Colour sits inside the palette above. No logos, no trend pieces, no festive or bridal styling, no gym wear outside the track image, and even there nothing branded or technical-looking.

## 1.7 Photography

Medium-format rendering throughout, with the lens and camera height chosen per picture: 35–45 mm with shift for architecture, 55–80 mm for figures in rooms, 90–110 mm for the still life and the portrait. Verticals are straight in every architectural frame. Grain is fine and organic. Retouching restraint is part of the look: pores, lines, grey hair and creased cloth stay.

## 1.8 Sequence on the homepage

Hero (dark) → **care-walk** (bright, wide) with **care-meal** (dark, graphic) and **care-rest** (dusk) → **progress-stride** (darkest, vertical) → Reveal Lens (coded, optional macro) → **feeling-fine** (brightest) → Scene 05 and 06 (coded) → **journey** trio (cool studio, warm colonnade, gentle portrait) → Scene 08 (coded) → **portal-her** and **portal-him** (first appearance of garnet and teal).

# Part 5 — Quality-control scorecard

Score each generated image before uploading it. 0 = fails, 1 = weak, 2 = acceptable, 3 = excellent.

| # | Criterion | What to look at | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Composition lock | Subject position and every text-safe zone match the brief | /3 |
| 2 | Believable light | One source, correct direction, shadows consistent | /3 |
| 3 | Face and skin | Specific face; pores, lines, real hair at 200% | /3 |
| 4 | Anatomy | Hands, feet, teeth, eyes, joins between people | /3 |
| 5 | Architecture and geometry | Straight verticals, regular steps or piers, plausible joints | /3 |
| 6 | Materials | Cloth has weight; stone, concrete, timber, metal read as themselves | /3 |
| 7 | Colour discipline | Inside the palette; amber bounded; no wash | /3 |
| 8 | Wardrobe | As briefed, premium, no logos, creases natural | /3 |
| 9 | Restraint | No haze, glow, particles, glamour or stock expression | /3 |
| 10 | Campaign fit | Sits beside the approved portal-her without looking like another shoot | /3 |

**Pass:** 24 or more out of 30, with no criterion at 0, and none of the image’s own reject conditions present.
**Automatic rejection, whatever the score:** any text or logo; a finger, limb or eye error; skin smoothing; a face that looks like a previous subject; anything implying the person is a patient or customer; anything medical.

After I integrate an image I add an eleventh check you cannot do from the file alone: how it looks in its slot at 1440 and 390 beside the type and the glass controls. An image can pass the scorecard and still be held back at that step; I will say why.
