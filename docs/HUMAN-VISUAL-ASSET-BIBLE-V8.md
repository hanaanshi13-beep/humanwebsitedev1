# Part 1 — Creative direction

## 1.1 The campaign idea: "The Quiet Interior"

HUMAN is about what is inside a person and not yet visible. The campaign never illustrates that with anatomy. It uses **interiors**: thresholds, courts, stairs, verandahs, rooms where light arrives through something (a silk sheer, a carved screen, ribbed glass, a skylight, a glass of water, the surface of a pool). In every picture, light has passed through or bounced off a material before it reaches the subject. That is the single recurring motif, and it is the photographic twin of the Reveal Lens on the site.

The setting language is **Indian modernism and its restored predecessors**: board-formed concrete, exposed brick, lime plaster, Kota and Kadappa stone, red oxide floors, terrazzo, teak, ash, cane, bell metal, bronze. These are real materials with visible joints, wear and repair. Nothing is a showroom.

## 1.2 Colour system

| Role | Colour | Where it is allowed to live |
|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | Rich charcoal #0B0D0F to basalt | Concrete in shadow, stone, deep shade |
| Ground (light) | Natural ivory #F6F3EC | Lime plaster, pale concrete, cotton, terrazzo |
| Accent | Champagne amber, restrained | One element per image at most: a sun blade, bell metal, a lamp |
| Her world | Garnet #54263B, mulberry, oxblood | Pigmented plaster, dyed silk, red oxide floor |
| His world | Deep ocean teal #164846, slate | Dyed linen, tinted plaster, stone, water-light |
| Skin | Neutral-warm, true to the individual | Never tinted by the grade |

Rules: colour is carried by materials, never by a wash or a gel. Amber appears as one bounded element and is absent from four of the ten images. Garnet and teal appear only in the two portals; the story images stay in charcoal, ivory and natural material colour so the portals arrive as an event.

## 1.3 Light

Faces are lit to be looked at: directional, sculpting cheekbone and jaw, with a catchlight in the eyes and rich but controlled contrast. Every such light still has a physical cause in the room. One motivated source per picture, named in the brief, with its direction and what it has passed through. Fill comes only from surfaces in the room. No rim light without a physical cause. No haze, no visible rays, no glow behind a head. Shadows keep detail and take their colour from the nearest surface.

## 1.5 Casting

**Direction.** The campaign cast are exceptionally photogenic fictional Indian women and men, mostly 22 to 32: expressive, distinctive faces with strong features, beautiful natural skin and compelling eyes; stylish, confident and contemporary. Hair is professionally styled and grooming is refined. The reference point is a luxury fashion editorial, with the immediacy of the best editorial photography on Instagram. Attractive never means filtered, sexualised or exaggerated: skin keeps its texture, bodies are believable, clothing is modest.

**Gaze.** Three subjects look straight into the lens, because eye contact is what stops a viewer in the first second: both portals and the young professional. The other images stay candid so they keep telling the story of an ordinary healthy day.

## 1.6 Wardrobe

Fashion-forward, premium and modest, with excellent fit. Tailoring in silk crêpe, linen-silk, khadi-silk and wool crêpe, including tonal sets and a blazer worn over the shoulders; knitwear in merino and cotton-silk; understated luxury activewear in heavyweight cotton, fine merino jersey and matte technical twill. Jewellery is small and sculptural (an ear cuff, fine stacked rings, a thin chain). Each garment is described by cut, cloth and how it creases. Colour sits inside the palette. No logos, no festive or bridal styling, no watches, no bare chests, no plunging necklines or slits, no compression wear.

## 1.7 Photography

Medium-format rendering throughout, with the lens and camera height chosen per picture: 35–45 mm with shift for architecture, 55–80 mm for figures in rooms, 90–110 mm for the still life and the portrait. Verticals are straight in every architectural frame. Grain is fine and organic. Retouching restraint is part of the look: pores, fine skin texture, flyaway hairs and creased cloth stay.

## 1.8 Lifestyle

Affluence is carried by the places and the making, never by objects that announce money. The settings are architect-designed private residences (the doorway, the water court, the courtyard dining room, the daybed room, the screened gallery, the kitchen counter), a private wellness building (the track, the stair, the pool loggia) and a design studio. What reads as expensive is board-marked concrete poured well, a teak screen with even fins, hand-beaten bell metal, a stone counter, tailored cloth. Excluded everywhere: cars, logos, watches as status, mansions, chandeliers, marble lobbies, displayed jewellery.

## 1.9 Sequence on the homepage

Hero (dark) → **care-walk** (bright, wide) with **care-meal** (dark, graphic) and **care-rest** (dusk) → **progress-stride** (darkest, vertical) → Reveal Lens (coded, optional macro) → **feeling-fine** (brightest) → Scene 05 and 06 (coded) → **journey** trio (cool studio, warm gallery, gentle portrait) → Scene 08 (coded) → **portal-her** and **portal-him** (first appearance of garnet and teal).

**03 — Subject casting.** Fictional Indian woman, 25–30. Tall and athletic-slim, with a toned arm and upright, easy posture. Luminous deep warm-brown skin with real texture. **04 — Wardrobe and styling.** A sleeveless high-neck column dress in heavy matte garnet silk crêpe (#54263B), cut close through the body and falling straight. Over her shoulders, worn as a cape, a sharply tailored blazer in the same garnet cloth with a strong shoulder line; one hand holds its lapel edge. One sculptural gold ear cuff and a slim hammered-gold cuff on the wrist. Grooming: softly defined eyes, groomed brows, natural lip, skin left with its natural sheen and texture. Excluded: necklace, scarf, bridal or festive styling, heavy make-up, nail colour, plunging necklines, slits.

**05 — Environment and set design.** A restored early-modern Indian house. A deep doorway, wall about 45 cm thick, in hand-trowelled lime plaster pigmented oxblood-mulberry, with visible trowel movement and a slight burnish. Door frame in old oiled teak. A brass lever handle with worn lacquer. Floor in polished dark red oxide with hairline craquelure. Through the doorway to the right, a brighter room with a tall narrow window behind an undyed raw-silk sheer. No furniture, no ornaments.

**06 — Composition and framing.** Vertical 3:4. She leans with her back and left shoulder against the teak door frame in the right 55% of the frame, three-quarter length (head to mid-thigh), body angled toward the bright room, head turned back to the camera. Top of head 12% from the top; eyes 26–30% down and 60–64% across. Her right hand holds the lapel at chest height. The left 34% is uninterrupted dark plaster. The bottom 36% falls to deep shadow: dress and floor in low values, nothing bright. The site crops about 4% off the top and 9% off the bottom on desktop, and about 10% off the left and 7% off the right on mobile, so head and hand stay inside the central zone. Live text sits bottom-left.

**07 — Camera direction.** Medium format, 80 mm lens (about 63 mm full-frame). Camera level at her sternum height, 2.6 m away, verticals straight. f/4. Focus on the near eye; plaster texture beside her stays legible, the far room goes soft.

**08 — Lighting design.** Key: daylight from the tall window in the far room, diffused by the silk sheer, arriving from camera-right and slightly behind her. With her head turned to the lens it lights the right side of her face and sculpts cheekbone, jaw and collarbone, and lays one soft-edged vertical band of light on the plaster behind her shoulder. Fill: a pale lime-plastered wall out of frame to the left returns a low soft fill, about two stops under, so the shadow side stays open and both eyes carry a catchlight. 5200 K. No lamp, no rim from a second source.

**09 — Colour grading.** Rich, controlled contrast. Blacks sit at deep aubergine, not zero. The dress is slightly more saturated than the wall so the cloth leads. Skin neutral-warm with true brown undertones, luminous on the lit side and matte in shadow. No added vignette.

**10 — Microdetails.** Trowel marks catching the light band; one hairline crack in the plaster; the dry sheen of silk crêpe along a fold; the blazer’s canvas holding its shoulder; fine flyaway hairs lit at the temple; a clear catchlight in each eye; a scuff on the brass handle; the window as a faint soft bar reflected in the polished floor at far right.
